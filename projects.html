Australian Research Council (ARC) Funding

  • ARC Discovery Project DP260104188
    Reduce Hallucination in Large Language Models via Knowledge-based Reasoning
    Funding: AUD $611,454.00 · Role: Chief Investigator
    Period: January 2026 – December 2028

    This project develops knowledge-based reasoning techniques to detect and reduce hallucinations in large language models, with a focus on news fact-checking. By integrating external factual knowledge, it aims to improve the reliability of AI-generated information and help combat misinformation.

Industry Support

  • CSIRO Industry PhD Program (iPhD), 2026 Round 1
    Responsible AI-assisted Decision Making with Longitudinal Multi-modal Data in Precision Health
    Funding: AUD $206,793.00 · Role: Co-Principal Investigator
    Period: December 2025 – December 2030

    This project uses large language models to integrate and analyse longitudinal multi-modal health data for personalised lifestyle and mental wellness support. It aims to generate adaptive wellness recommendations that support preventative healthcare and improve individual wellbeing.

  • CSIRO Industry PhD Program (iPhD), 2025 Round 2
    Risk-Aware and Trustworthy AI Agents for Adaptive Quantitative Trading
    Funding: AUD $262,023.00 · Role: Co-Principal Investigator
    Period: June 2026 – June 2031

    This project develops risk-aware and trustworthy AI agents for adaptive quantitative trading by combining reinforcement learning, financial time-series modelling, and explainable AI. It aims to enable robust trading strategies, real-time risk management, and interpretable decisions under changing market conditions.
